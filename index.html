Privacy Policy for JobNest

Last Updated: November 27, 2026

Mallikarjun Avatagi ("we," "our," or "us") operates the JobNest mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when registering and using JobNest:

  • Account & Identity Information: Name, mobile phone number, email address, city/location, and account type (Job Seeker or Employer/Recruiter).
  • Professional & Profile Data: Educational qualifications, work experience, skills, expected salary, resume/CV files, and job preferences.
  • Employer & Job Listing Data: Company name, business contact details, job descriptions, and recruiter communication details.
  • Device & Technical Data: Basic device information, app version, crash logs, and push notification tokens used to deliver job alerts and maintain app security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly to:

  • Create and manage your JobNest user account.
  • Match job seekers with relevant job openings and share applicant profiles with employers when you apply for a job.
  • Enable direct communication between candidates and recruiters.
  • Send job alerts, application status updates, and important service notifications.
  • Verify listings, prevent fraud or spam, and improve app performance.

3. Sharing of Information

  • With Employers / Recruiters: When you apply for a job on JobNest, your profile details, contact information, and resume are shared with the respective employer so they can evaluate your application and contact you.
  • With Service Providers: We use trusted cloud infrastructure and authentication services (such as Google Firebase) to securely store data, authenticate users, and send notifications.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government authority in India.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell or rent your personal information to third-party marketers.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption and access controls to protect your personal data. We retain your account information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You may review, update, or delete your profile information directly within the JobNest app at any time. To request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data, contact us at arjun2599@gmail.com with your registered mobile number or email address. We will process deletion requests within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

JobNest is intended for individuals aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.
